01 / The finding

What WAMs need:
more video, fewer action labels.

Broad video coverage supplies task knowledge. A small amount of action supervision grounds it in execution, while labeling every video brings little additional benefit in these experiments.

More video matters. Full action labels are not required.

03 / The model

01

Unified pretraining

Robot trajectories and human egocentric video share a unified processing pipeline and a 46D action space. Before curation, the data collection includes approximately 13,976 hours of robot data and 14,128 hours of human egocentric data.

02

Dual-scale visual memory

Short-term observations capture local motion; long-term observations track task progress. Both provide temporal context beyond the current frame.

03

Directional attention

The action expert reads predicted visual dynamics. A structured attention mask connects video and action experts without feeding action tokens back into video generation.

04

Action forcing

Different actions within a training chunk receive different noise levels, allowing the action expert to use partially clean actions alongside future video features.

04 / Physical capabilities

Six tasks. Two robot platforms.

Long-horizon execution, precise placement and coordinated manipulation on AgiBot G2 and ARX AC-1.
